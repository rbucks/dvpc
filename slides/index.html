§ 01

This is a 4CD competition.

If you are enrolled in a Contra Costa Community College District course — any course, at any of our colleges — you are eligible to apply. One class is enough. Enroll, then apply.

THE THREE COLLEGES

  • Contra Costa College — San Pablo
  • Diablo Valley College — Pleasant Hill & San Ramon
  • Los Medanos College — Pittsburg & Brentwood
  • Districtwide Community Education

WHO THAT INCLUDES

  • Credit students, full-time or part-time
  • Dual-enrolled high school students
  • Community members in a non-credit class
  • No citizenship or status requirements

There are no tracks and no categories. Everyone fills out the same form and stands on the same stage — forms.cloud.microsoft/r/RB7gvjDQPZ

§ 02

Diablo Valley, not Silicon Valley.

A nail salon expansion and an AI product go into the same pile, in front of the same panel, scored against the same criteria. We are actively recruiting range.

MAIN STREET

  • Restaurants, food trucks, cafés, breweries
  • Salons, childcare, cleaning, repair shops
  • Landscaping, construction, and the trades
  • Retail, bookkeeping, and local services

SCIENCE & PRODUCT

  • Biotech and health tech
  • Software, apps, and AI
  • Aviation and industry
  • Hardware and climate

No technical co-founder, prototype, or growth chart required.

§ 09

The rules, in brief.

Who can enter?Anyone with one active 4CD enrollment — any course, credit or non-credit Community Education.
What do I submit?A short description of the business idea and a slide presentation of no more than 15 slides. No video.
How big can a team be?No more than five people. Solo founders are permitted.
Can I pitch a business I already run?Yes, if it has less than $25,000 in total assets or annual revenue.
I entered before. Can I come back?Yes — including past winners. The new submission must be a different concept.
Can I submit more than once?Yes. But a finalist pitches only once at the live event, solo or on a team.
Do I keep my idea?100% of your venture, IP, and equity. The competition takes no equity and no fees.
Can the public come?Yes. The final is free, and attendance requires no enrollment of any kind.
